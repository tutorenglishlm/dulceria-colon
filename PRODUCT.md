# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to Mérida, Yucatán and locals of all ages looking for a traditional treat — families, tourists walking Paseo de Montejo or the Centro Histórico, and multi-generational Yucatecos for whom the shop is a lifelong ritual. They arrive on mobile, often mid-outing, wanting to know what to order, where the nearest branch is, and that this is the real, historic place.

## Product Purpose

Marketing/landing site for **Dulcería y Sorbetería Colón**, a historic sorbetería in Mérida founded **1907** by Sr. Vicente Rodríguez Peláez. The site exists to convey more than a century of tradition, showcase the tropical-fruit sorbets and the signature **champola**, and help visitors find and visit a branch. Success = a visitor understands the heritage, wants the product, and knows exactly where to go.

## Positioning

Over 100 years of continuous tradition using the founder's original recipes — a living institution "de las tradiciones más arraigadas entre los yucatecos." The champola (milk + guanábana sorbet) is a signature only this house is known for. Authenticity and lineage are the moat; this is not a trendy new ice-cream brand.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Dulcería y Sorbetería Colón**. Tagline: "más de 100 años de tradición."
- Founder: **Sr. Vicente Rodríguez Peláez, 1907** (with friend Felipe Sánchez per prior research).
- Signature products: sorbetes de fruta tropical (coco, guanábana, mamey), the **champola**, and pastelitos/dulces individuales.
- Logo asset: `images/logo.png`.
- A prior pastel/terracotta redesign was rejected by the client ("aesthetic is wrong"); the authentic brand reads as heritage — charcoal + gold + cream on elcolon.mx.

## Product Principles

1. **Lineage is the product.** Every surface should feel like it has been there since 1907, not decorated to look old.
2. **The champola is the hero.** It is the one thing only this house owns; give it pride of place.
3. **Get them to a branch.** The visitor is often mid-outing on mobile; branch + phone must always be one tap away.
4. **Never invent facts.** Hours, prices, addresses, and delivery links are unknown until the client provides them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish-language, multi-generational audience including older visitors; favor legible type, high contrast, and large tap targets.
